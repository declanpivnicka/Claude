Questions owners ask us

Straight answers

How do you get paid?

We take an agreed percentage of the business, paid out of the sale. There are no monthly fees or consulting bills. If the business isn’t worth more, we don’t do well either, so our interests line up with yours.

Do I lose control of my company?

No. You stay the owner and make the final calls. We work alongside you and your team, and every major change is agreed with you first.

What happens to my employees and customers?

They’re the reason the business is valuable. We train your people, give them better tools and look for buyers who will keep the team and keep serving your customers well.

I’m not good with computers. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We set up the software, train the office and crews, and keep it simple. You’ll get a short weekly report you can read on paper if you like.

How long does this take?

Most of the work happens over 12 to 24 months. Some owners sell sooner, some stay on longer to grow further. You decide when to sell.

Is our conversation confidential?

Yes. We’re happy to sign a confidentiality agreement before you share any numbers. Your employees and customers won’t hear about it from us.
